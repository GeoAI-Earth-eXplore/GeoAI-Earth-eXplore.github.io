About

What is GEX?

GEX stands for GeoAI Earth eXplore.

GEX is an open resource hub designed to make high-quality GeoAI resources easier to discover, organize, and share.

The project covers Earth observation, remote sensing, GIS, geospatial artificial intelligence, foundation models, spatial analysis, environmental applications, and emerging GeoAI technologies.
